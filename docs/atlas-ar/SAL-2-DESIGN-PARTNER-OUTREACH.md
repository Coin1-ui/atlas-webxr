## Who to target (MiroFish + ICP)

### Priority 1 — Elena profile (retail / home)

**Titles:** Head of e-commerce, Digital director, Omnichannel lead  
**Company:** Regional furniture, DTC sofa/bed, kitchen showroom, rental staging  
**Triggers:** Returns citing wrong size; failed AR pilot; Shopify Plus evaluating 3D  
**MiroFish hook:** ~30% “too complex” → lead with **≤15 min live placement**, not feature list  
**Tier to pitch:** Starter $5 proof → convert to **design partner $59** when 3+ SKUs ready

### Priority 2 — Marcus profile (field sales)

**Titles:** VP Sales, Sales ops, Regional sales director  
**Company:** Fixtures, equipment, medical devices, industrial components  
**Triggers:** Reps still on PDFs; IT blocked custom AR app  
**MiroFish hook:** Unlimited reps vs per-seat showroom tools  
**Tier to pitch:** Starter $5 one product line → Launch $59 when catalog curated

### Do not pursue (anti-ICP)

- Shopify embed-only (Lisa) — redirect to plugins or require workspace use case  
- Native iOS app mandate · shared multi-user AR · game/VFX pipelines

## Win theme (use in every touch)

> **Same floor AR outcome as showroom tools that cost $99–450/mo — self-serve in an afternoon, from $5/mo, no per-seat tax on reps.**

**MiroFish objection prep:**

| Objection (~sim weight) | First response |
|-------------------------|----------------|
| Too complex / unclear value (30%) | “15-minute test: one GLB on **your** floor on this call.” |
| Launch/Growth expensive (25%) | “**$5** Starter or **$59** design-partner Growth — not $179.” |
| Viewer-only / weak interactivity (20%) | Live demo: cyan floor ring, reposition, 3D inspect same session |
| Security & privacy (late) | IT one-pager: tenant isolation, HTTPS, no shopper login |
| CFO ROI (budget gate) | One avoided $2k return ≈ 34 months of Launch |

## Outreach sequence (5–7 touches / 14 days)

Based on B2B design-partner playbooks: plain text, workflow-specific, 50–125 words on cold email, ask for **15 minutes** first.

| Day | Channel | Action |
|-----|---------|--------|
| 0 | Email | Workflow question (Template A) |
| 2 | LinkedIn | Connect + 3-line brief (Template B) |
| 5 | Email | Value proof — `/demo` + 30 sec Loom optional (Template C) |
| 8 | LinkedIn DM | Scarcity — “2 of 3 slots left” (Template D) |
| 11 | Email | Breakup / soft close (Template E) |
| 14 | Email | Founding 10 fallback if no DP fit (Template F) |

**Volume benchmark (Allston Labs):** ~250 personalized emails/week if full-time outbound; expect ~2 qualified DP conversations per 500 targeted sends.

## Discovery call script (15 min → 30 min expand)

**Minutes 0–3 — Hook (MiroFish: show, don't tell)**  
Share screen: `/demo` on phone or theirs. One placement. No deck yet.

**Minutes 3–8 — Problem fit**  
Ask ([SALES-PLAYBOOK.md](./SALES-PLAYBOOK.md) MEDDPICC-lite):

1. How many SKUs need AR in 90 days?  
2. Who uploads 3D today?  
3. Android vs iPhone mix for buyers?  
4. What would prove value in the **first 10 minutes**?

**Minutes 8–12 — Offer**  
“If we hit **50 sessions with placements** in 90 days, would you be open to a short quote? In return: Growth at **$59**, biweekly feedback, founder line to engineering.”

**Minutes 12–15 — Close**  
Book **setup call** within 48h: upload **their** GLB, live `/w/slug` test.  
Send: IT security bullets from slide 9 + [PRICING-FEATURE-READINESS.md](./PRICING-FEATURE-READINESS.md) internal cheat sheet (not customer-facing).

## Design partner agreement (one email / MOU)

Paste into doc; both parties sign or confirm by reply.

**Parties:** Omni Manual Private Limited (Atlas AR) and [Company legal name]  
**Term:** 90 days from [start date]  
**Purpose:** Pilot browser floor AR for [use case — e.g. sofa catalog on customer floor]

**Scope**

- In scope: 1 workspace, up to 100 GLB models (Growth limits), branded `/w/[slug]`, Chrome + Safari AR  
- Out of scope: custom native apps, SSO, custom domain, multi-workspace (Scale — separate quote)

**Success criteria**

- ≥50 AR sessions/month with ≥1 placement each, measured via workspace usage dashboard  
- First catalog SKU live within 15 minutes of guided onboarding call

**Commercials**

- **$59/mo** for Growth-tier limits during pilot  
- On success + conversion within 90 days of pilot end: **15% off annual** prepay OR Founding rate locked 12 months  
- Overage: soft warnings in-app; usage beyond included meters is billed automatically with the subscription payment cycle (disclose pack rates on `/pricing`)

**Partner commitments**

- Biweekly 30-min feedback call  
- One internal champion for upload + link distribution  
- Testimonial quote + logo if success criteria met (subject to approval)

**Atlas commitments**

- Founder/product access on feedback calls  
- Enable Growth limits + session log export toggle on request  
- Priority bug response during pilot

**Confidentiality:** Mutual; no customer PII required for shoppers (viewers need no account)  
**Termination:** Either party, 7 days notice  
**Data:** Workspace-scoped storage; deletion on request
