## Discovery questions (ask *before* each slide)

| Slide | Ask first |
|-------|-----------|
| 1 | "When a buyer is unsure about fit or size, what do they do today — leave, ask an associate, or buy and hope?" |
| 2 | "What does a size-related return cost you all-in — shipping, restocking, lost margin?" |
| 3 | "If your team could share one branded link and place any SKU on the floor tomorrow, what would you put in it first?" |
| 4 | "Who owns the 3D files today — merchandising, a vendor, or nobody?" |
| 5 | "How do showroom staff explain scale today — tape measure, guess, or skip it?" |
| 6 | "What do reps carry into a site visit besides the conversation — PDF, sample, nothing?" |
| 7 | "Have you looked at a showroom SaaS or a store plugin already — what stopped you?" |
| 8 | "Is this a pilot budget, a line-item for this quarter, or a strategic platform decision?" |
| 9 | "Who besides you needs to bless data handling — IT, legal, or procurement?" |
| 10 | "What date should your first SKU be live on a customer's floor — this week or next?" *(then offer four paths: Starter, live upload, Design partner, Founding 10)* |

## Additional objection handlers

**"We already have photos and video on the site."**  
"Photos don't transfer to *their* room at *their* ceiling height. AR closes the gap between inspiration and commitment — especially on sofas and beds. Keep photos; add one link for high-AOV SKUs."

**"Our IT queue is 6 months."**  
"Shoppers never install anything — HTTPS link only. Admin is a web login; no MDM, no app store. Priya usually cares about tenant isolation and no shopper PII — we can send the one-pager before you upload a single file."
