## Before you start (60 seconds)

1. **Know your persona** — Retail (Elena) → emphasize slides 5, 7, 8. Field (Marcus) → slides 6, 7, 8. IT in room → linger slide 9.
2. **Open tabs:** Interactive deck · [Live demo `/demo`](https://main.d7vfdpujdozkj.amplifyapp.com/demo) · your workspace `/w/{slug}` if you have one.
3. **Set the frame:** “I’ll walk through ten slides — about fifteen minutes — then we’ll place one product on your floor if you’re on your phone. Sound good?”
4. **Press N** in the web deck to show speaker notes if needed.

## Slide 1 — Title · ~90 sec

**[ASK — before you advance past title]**  
“When a buyer is unsure about fit or size, what do they do today — leave, ask an associate, or buy and hope?”

**[SAY]**  
“Thanks for making time. I’m not here to talk about ‘AR technology’ in the abstract. I’m here because every team in your category loses deals and absorbs returns at the exact moment scale matters — when the customer is standing in the room asking, *will this actually fit?*

Atlas AR answers that question with one **branded link**. Your catalog, your logo, your floor — in **Chrome or Safari**. No app store. No six-month IT queue.

The headline on this slide is the whole point: **See it on their floor before they buy it.** Everything else is how we make that true in under ten minutes.”

**[PAUSE]** — let them answer your opening question briefly.

**[TRANSITION → 2]**  
“You’ve probably felt the cost of that uncertainty. Let’s name it.”

---

## Slide 2 — Problem · ~2 min

**[ASK]**  
“What does a size-related return cost you all-in — shipping, restocking, lost margin?”

**[SAY]**  
“Static catalogs fail at the moment of decision. That’s not a marketing line — it’s what your associates and reps live with.

Photos lie about scale. A sofa that looks perfect online shrinks or dominates in the room. High-AOV returns follow.

Field teams still walk in with **PDFs and decks** while the buyer is literally standing in the space where the product should go.

Meanwhile showroom AR vendors put you through **$99 to $450 a month** contracts and a sales cycle before you see your *own* SKU on a floor.

And custom AR apps? **Six figures**, security review, MDM — projects that die in quarter three.

We’re not saying AR is new. We’re saying the **path to your floor** shouldn’t require a project.”

**[IF they say ‘we tried AR’]**  
“Was the failure floating furniture, app install friction, or IT backlog? — jot mentally; you’ll answer on slide 3 or 5.”

**[TRANSITION → 3]**  
“Here’s the simpler model.”

---

## Slide 3 — Solution · ~2 min

**[ASK]**  
“If your team could share one branded link and place any SKU on the floor tomorrow, what would you put in it first?”

**[SAY]**  
“**One workspace. One branded link. Real floor AR.**

You upload GLB models once from desktop admin — merchandising or ops, not engineering.

You share **`/w/your-brand`** — shoppers and reps open it like any HTTPS link. No login for viewers. No app store.

**Chrome and Safari** — floor placement *and* 3D inspect in the same session.

And unlike seat-based tools: **unlimited** reps and viewers on every plan.

This is not a single-store plugin embedded in one website. It’s **your** white-label workspace — showroom **and** field from the same catalog.”

**[DEMO TEASE]**  
“Hold that SKU in mind — we’ll place it on slide 4.”

**[TRANSITION → 4]**  
“Three steps. Most teams live in under ten minutes.”

## Slide 4 — How it works · ~2–3 min (+ optional live demo)

**[ASK]**  
“Who owns the 3D files today — merchandising, a vendor, or nobody?”

**[SAY]**  
“**Step one — Upload.** GLB plus icon from PC. iPhone gets USDZ automatically.

**Step two — Brand.** Logo, accent color, slug ` /w/your-brand `.

**Step three — Share and place.** Customer or rep taps **Start AR**, scans the floor, places at true scale.

That’s the whole operating model. Admin on PC; everyone else on a link.”

**[DEMO — 3–5 min if engaged]**  
“Pull out your phone. Open **[demo URL]** /demo. Pick any model. Tap Start AR. I’ll stay quiet while you scan your floor — tell me when the ring turns cyan.”

**[COACH NOTE]** Time-box demo. If &gt;5 min or friction, say: “Onboarding we target first placement in fifteen minutes — I’ll send a checklist.”

**[TRANSITION → 5 or 6]**  
Pick **one** ICP slide based on buyer:

- Retail / e-comm → Slide 5  
- Field / B2B → Slide 6  
- Mixed room → Slide 5, skip 6 verbally: “Same platform for field — I’ll send Marcus’s one-pager.”

## Slide 5 — Retail ICP (Elena) · ~2 min

**[ASK]**  
“How do showroom staff explain scale today — tape measure, guess, or skip it?”

**[SAY]**  
“For **furniture and home retail** — Elena’s world — the job is simple: stop returns that say *wrong size* and give associates something credible on the floor.

QR on tags or a link from an associate. Customer places the sofa, bed, or table at **true floor scale** — not floating on the coffee table.

Atlas **locks to floor planes**. Cyan ring means placeable; you’re not selling a gimmick that drifts.

Start with **Starter at $5** for five SKUs. Move to **Launch at $59** when the showroom is fully live.

One avoided $2k return pays for years of Launch — but don’t do that math out loud yet; we’ll get to pricing.”

**[OBJECTION — floating AR]**  
“You’re right to be skeptical. Our placement pipeline is session-tuned for floor height. That’s why the demo you just tried matters more than this slide.”

**[TRANSITION → 7]** — skip 6 if retail-only  
“Whether you’re retail or field, buyers always compare us to plugins and showroom SaaS. Here’s the honest table.”

## Slide 6 — Field ICP (Marcus) · ~2 min

**[ASK]**  
“What do reps carry into a site visit besides the conversation — PDF, sample, nothing?”

**[SAY]**  
“For **B2B field sales** — Marcus’s team — the pain is credibility in the customer’s **facility**, not yours.

Reps share **approved SKUs only**. Central catalog control from admin; no rogue files on personal phones.

The buyer sees the model **in their bay, plant, or lobby** during the visit — not back at HQ on a monitor.

**Session analytics** tell ops which models actually get placed — not just which decks got emailed.

No six-figure custom app. No MDM rollout. Link in email or SMS.

**Starter at $5** pilots one product line; **Launch at $59** when the catalog grows.”

**[OBJECTION — rep adoption]**  
“Reps won’t install anything. They tap a link. You control the catalog; they share and view.”

**[TRANSITION → 7]**  
“Here’s how we compare to the alternatives you’ve probably already googled.”

---

## Slide 7 — Comparison · ~2 min

**[ASK]**  
“Have you looked at a showroom SaaS or a store plugin already — what stopped you?”

**[SAY]**  
“Three columns. **Store plugins** — one embed, one site, dev integration, per-site pricing.

**Showroom SaaS** — often excellent AR, but gated demos, **$99–450 a month**, and their brand on the contract.

**Atlas AR** — **your brand**, **your catalog**, **unlimited** viewers and reps, **from $5 self-serve**, live **same day**.

We’re not claiming every feature showroom incumbents built over a decade. We’re claiming the **outcome you need** — floor scale in the buyer’s space — without the procurement theater.

For finance: one avoided **$2,000 return** is roughly **thirty-four months** of Launch at $59. One saved custom-app quote pays for a decade.”

**[TRANSITION → 8]**  
“Concrete numbers on the next slide.”

---

## Slide 8 — Pricing · ~2 min

**[ASK]**  
“Is this a pilot budget, a line-item for this quarter, or a strategic platform decision?”

**[SAY]**  
“**Starter — $5 a month.** Five models, hundred sessions. Proof, not a science project.

**Launch — $59.** Where most showrooms and pilot teams land — thirty models.

**Growth — $179.** When IT and analytics need to sign off — hundred models, full usage picture.

**Scale — from $499** for multi-brand operators.

Two offers worth naming: **14-day Growth trial**, no card — we enable Growth limits on your workspace. **Design partner** — three slots, ninety days Growth at **$59/mo** with biweekly feedback. **Founding ten** — Growth at Launch price for twelve months without the formal partner program.

Lead months one through three with **Starter**. Never apologize for five dollars — it’s the wedge that gets the first SKU on a real floor.”

**[COACH NOTE]** Match tier to their answer. Pilot → Starter. Team rollout → Launch. IT/security in process → Growth trial or design partner. Price objection → $59 partner rate, not $179.

**[TRANSITION → 9]** — if Priya or IT present, slow down. If not, 60 sec max.  
“Buying committees ask about data. Slide nine is for Priya.”

---

## Slide 9 — Security & IT · ~90 sec–2 min

**[ASK]**  
“Who besides you needs to bless data handling — IT, legal, or procurement?”

**[SAY]**  
“Built for buying committees.

**Tenant isolation** — separate catalog, branding, and usage per workspace.

**HTTPS-only** delivery. Shoppers **never create accounts** — no PII collection on the view path.

Admin uses **Cognito JWT**; catalog lives in workspace-scoped storage.

No native app → **no app-store MDM** project.

I’ll send a one-pager you can forward to IT. If Priya’s on the call, what would she need to see that isn’t on this slide?”

**[TRANSITION → 10]**  
“Last slide — what we do **this week**, not someday.”

---

## Slide 10 — CTA · ~2 min

**[ASK]**  
“What date should your first SKU be live on a customer’s floor — this week or next?”

**[SAY]**  
“Four paths — pick one before we hang up.

**One — Sign up Starter at $5.** Upload five SKUs; share your link internally by Friday.

**Two — Stay on this call, open `/demo`,** then I create your workspace and we upload **your** GLB together.

**Three — Design partner.** We have **three slots**. Ninety days of **Growth limits at $59/mo** — not $179. **Biweekly thirty-minute feedback.** Success metric: **fifty-plus AR sessions per month** with at least one floor placement each. Case study quote if we hit it; **fifteen percent off annual** if you convert.

**Four — Founding ten.** Growth features at Launch price for **twelve months** — less structured feedback, same **$59** rate.

I'm not asking for a strategy project. I’m asking for **one model on one real floor** with a date attached.

**[CLOSE — Proposal Strategist]**  
“What’s the one SKU that would make this a win for you? … Who besides you needs to say yes? … What should I send in the follow-up email — IT one-pager, design partner MOU, or demo link?”

**[DATED NEXT STEP — mandatory]**  
“Let’s book **twenty minutes** to review your `/w/slug` link live — or **hold a design partner slot through [date]**. Does Tuesday 10am or 2pm work?”
