# Atlas AR — Ideal customer profiles (v1)

## ICP 1: Furniture & home retail

**Who:** Regional furniture chains, DTC sofa/bed brands, kitchen showrooms, rental staging companies.

**Job to be done:** Let shoppers see **true scale** of products on their floor before buying — without app install on Android; iOS via Quick Look.

**Pain today:**
- Static photos don't convey size
- Showroom visits are costly
- Competitors use generic AR apps with poor floor locking

**Atlas AR fit:**
- Upload catalog GLBs once (admin on PC)
- Sales associate sends link or QR on showroom floor
- Floor scan + placement (proven on Android Chrome)
- Object mode for close inspection

**Buyer:** Head of digital / e-commerce manager  
**User:** Store staff + end customer on phone  
**Deal size:** Pro tier ($299/mo) + implementation help

**Success metric:** ↑ conversion on high-AOV items; ↓ returns citing "wrong size"

---

## ICP 2: B2B field sales

**Who:** Manufacturers selling large equipment, fixtures, medical devices, industrial components to buyers on-site.

**Job to be done:** Rep places **approved 3D model** in customer's space during visit — credible, branded, no IT project.

**Pain today:**
- PowerPoint and PDF don't land in physical space
- Custom AR apps cost $100k+ and stall in IT
- Generic viewers don't lock floor height reliably

**Atlas AR fit:**
- Company-branded workspace
- Curated model library per product line
- Session logs for sales ops (which models shown)

**Buyer:** VP Sales + marketing ops  
**User:** Field rep (Android phone)  
**Deal size:** Pro → Enterprise (multi-region teams)

**Success metric:** Shorter sales cycle; more demos → proposals

---

## Anti-ICP (do not sell v1 to)

- Game studios / VFX (wrong product)
- Customers requiring **native iOS AR app only** (we are Quick Look + WebXR)
- AR on desktop/laptop without phone
- Real-time multi-user shared AR session (not MVP)
