# Privacy Policy

**Effective:** 21 May 2026  
**Controller:** Omni Manual Private Limited (Atlas AR)

This Privacy Policy explains how Atlas AR processes personal data when you visit our websites, create an account, use browser-based AR features, or interact with a customer workspace powered by Atlas AR. This Policy is designed to meet transparency expectations under the EU/UK GDPR, California CPRA, Canada’s PIPEDA, India’s DPDPA, and comparable laws.

> **Important:** This Policy describes our current practices. It is not legal advice.

## 1. Who we are

Atlas AR is operated by **Omni Manual Private Limited** (“Atlas AR”, “we”, “us”). We are the data controller for account, billing, and platform operations data. For end-customers who only open a tenant showroom link without an Atlas account, the workspace owner may act as an independent controller; we process data as a processor according to their instructions and this Policy.

- Privacy: privacy@atlas-ar.com  
- Grievance officer (India DPDPA): grievance@atlas-ar.com  

## 2. Personal data we collect

Account data, billing data, usage and analytics (including AR session events), camera/motion processing on-device for AR placement, Customer Content metadata, support communications, and cookies as described in-app.

## 3. Purposes and legal bases

We process data to provide and secure the Service, authenticate users, measure usage and billing, send service notices, comply with law, and—with consent where required—marketing.

## 4. Your rights

Depending on your location, you may have access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, portability, and consent withdrawal rights. Submit requests to privacy@atlas-ar.com.
