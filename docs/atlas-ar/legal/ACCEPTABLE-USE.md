# Acceptable Use Policy

**Effective:** 21 May 2026  
**Entity:** Omni Manual Private Limited (Atlas AR)

This Acceptable Use Policy (“AUP”) governs use of Atlas AR services. It supplements our Terms of Service.

## 1. Permitted use

Use the Service to showcase products you are authorized to sell or demonstrate, train field teams, and operate branded showrooms for legitimate commercial purposes in compliance with law.

## 2. Prohibited conduct

You must not upload malware or content you lack rights to distribute; harass or impersonate others; bypass plan limits or security; scrape or overload the Service; use AR in private spaces without required consent; process special-category personal data without lawful basis; or resell the Service without a written partner agreement.

## 3. AR and camera responsibilities

Browser-based AR requires camera access on the user’s device. Provide clear notice to your customers and obtain permissions required in your jurisdiction. Users should remain aware of their physical surroundings while using AR.

## 4. Content standards

Customer Content must comply with applicable advertising, consumer protection, and sector rules. You are solely responsible for claims made through your AR experiences.

## 5. Enforcement

We may investigate complaints, remove content, throttle usage, or suspend accounts. Report abuse to legal@atlas-ar.com.
