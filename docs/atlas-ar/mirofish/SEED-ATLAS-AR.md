## Product summary (2026-05 — live product)

Atlas AR is a white-label, browser-based floor AR platform for **furniture/home retail** and **B2B field sales**. Merchants upload GLB 3D models once, brand a workspace, and share `/w/{slug}` links. Shoppers and field reps place true-scale models on the real floor in **Chrome (Android WebXR)** or **Safari (browser-based AR)** — no native app install.

**In-session UX (Android):** AR bottom dock panel, **AR/3D slide toggle**, dimensions chip, JSON session log (owner can disable per customer). **3D inspect mode** rotates/zooms the placed catalog model without leaving the session.

**Marketing story:** Landing hero badges **Chrome · in-browser AR + 3D** and **Safari · in-browser AR + 3D**; side-by-side **PC admin → phone AR** diagram; dedicated **browser AR + 3D viewer** section; guided onboarding `/admin/get-started`.

**Owner controls:** Per-workspace toggles for JSON log download, **Start AR** (blocks AR entry), and **Run camera check** (independent).

**Pricing (v2):** Starter $5/mo · Launch $59/mo · Growth $179/mo · Scale from $499/mo · 14-day Growth trial (no card).

**Differentiators:** Floor-accurate placement (not table-plane float), unlimited viewers/reps (no per-seat), self-serve signup, tenant-isolated catalogs, session analytics.

**Anti-ICP:** Game studios, iOS-native-only AR requirements, desktop-only AR, real-time multi-user shared sessions.

## Market context (2026)

- Single-store AR plugins: $10–65/mo (one embed, not white-label workspace)
- Typical showroom SaaS: $99–450/mo (often demo-gated onboarding)
- Custom AR apps: $100k+ and 6–12 month builds
- Retail AR trend: fit confidence and return reduction on high-AOV furniture
